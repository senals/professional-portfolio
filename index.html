About

I'm Senal. I work in the defence and communications sector as a Through-Life Capability Manager, which means I look after a capability from the first requirement through to the day it is replaced. Day to day that is a mix of listening to the people who rely on it, working with the engineers who build and support it, and making sure the plan, the cost and the reality still line up.

I came to this through computer science and a dissertation in UX and usability testing, and that training still shapes how I work. I like to understand how people use a thing before deciding how it should change, and I would rather test an assumption than argue about it.

Skills

Stakeholder management
Keeping users, engineers, suppliers and decision-makers aligned, and making sure each of them hears what they need in terms that make sense to them.
Requirements and systems engineering
Turning what people need into clear, testable requirements, and tracing them through design, delivery and support.
UX and usability testing
Planning and running usability tests, then turning what people did and said into practical design changes.
Process design and improvement
Mapping how work really gets done, finding where it slows down, and designing a simpler way that people will keep using.
Commercial and financial capability
Building and challenging costs, understanding contracts, and weighing value over the whole life of a capability and not only the purchase price.
