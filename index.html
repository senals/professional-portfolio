About

I work in the Technical Services as a Business Manager, which entails ensuring our capabilities are meeting business needs, communicating company-wide, from the company owners, to the commercial team, to the project managers, to the engineers and specialists.

My passion for technology stemmed from gaming, where I began questioning how games ran, the stories they told and the thought process behind how they were designed. This paved my academic pathway, studying Computer Science at Cardiff University, where I achieved a First Class BSc (Hons). From my degree I could home in on my interests in the field; I developed a fascination in fintech, machine learning and cyber-security. You will see this from my projects below!

At work, I like to be someone anyone can approach, where people feel heard and acknowledged and still get what they came for, with efficiency and clarity. I enjoy interacting with people day-to-day, and pride myself on my ability to communicate and get things done.

What I Bring

Communication under pressure
Staying calm, clear and fair when the stakes are high and everyone wants something different. In practice: Across more than 20 customer test events, I've had last-minute change requests pushed at me mid-test. I've held the line on the test plan, explained why and kept the customer onside. Every event has been delivered successfully, with great feedback from both the customer and our own team.
Negotiation and commercial thinking
Looking for the deal where every party comes away better off, and thinking about value over the long term. In practice: I negotiated a partner discount with a software supplier that we pass on to our customers, so we win more work, the customer pays less and the supplier sells more. I also helped secure a five-year, multi-million euro customer contract, structured with clear line items so the customer can add future work quickly.
Building relationships
Finding new business and looking after the relationships that keep it coming back. In practice: I built a CRM to keep track of our customers and stakeholders, so no conversation, follow-up or opportunity gets lost.
Presenting and training
Explaining technical and commercial ideas clearly, whoever is in the room. In practice: I present to customers during test events, help deliver training to customer teams and present to our senior leadership.
Process design and testing
Building processes that are clear, repeatable and easy for the next person to pick up. In practice: I built our testing process from the ground up, following ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119, complete with templates, process maps and onboarding guides for new testers. I'm now uplifting our wider process documents, including customer requirements and systems engineering, to the same standard.
UX and usability testing
Watching how people really use things, and turning that into practical design changes. In practice: My dissertation centred on UX and usability testing: planning tests, observing users and turning findings into recommendations.
Working internationally
Comfortable on the road and quick to settle into new places. In practice: I travel regularly for work and I'm flexible about where the job takes me. It helps that I genuinely enjoy exploring new places and cultures.
Always learning
Staying curious about technology and business, and making time to grow outside the day job. In practice: I've gained the Microsoft SC-900 certification, keep learning through Udemy and I'm currently reading The Personal MBA by Josh Kaufman and The Almanack of Naval Ravikant.
