About

I work in the Technical Services as a Business Manager, which entails ensuring our capabilities are meeting business needs, communicating company-wide, from the company owners, to the commercial team, to the project managers, to the engineers and specialists.

My passion for technology stemmed from gaming, where I began questioning how games ran, the stories they told and the thought process behind how they were designed. Projects

  • Dissertation · First Class

    Designing Digital Interventions

    A mobile app (React Native) helping university students see what drinking costs them, not just what it does to their health. I surveyed 18 students and interviewed 7, then built and tested a working prototype.

    The key insight: 76.9% of students cared more about cost than health, so I led with money. The app scored 78.5 on the System Usability Scale, well above the industry average of 68, and its tree-growth visual scored 4.3/5 for engagement.
