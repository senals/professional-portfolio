Privacy

This is a personal website run by Senal Siriwardana, based in Wales, UK. It collects very little. This note explains what, and why.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, I receive the name, email address and message you enter. I use them only to read and reply to your message. I do not add you to any mailing list or share your details with anyone else.

Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this site, and forwarded to my email inbox. I delete messages once they are no longer needed.

Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter to count page views, so I can see which pages are visited. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not collect personal data and does not track you across other websites.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set cookies. If you use the light or dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. It is never sent to me.

Hosting

Like most web hosts, Netlify keeps short-term server logs that include visitors' IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you. Email me at senalbusy@gmail.com and I will sort it out. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
